# Jawbreak Technical Report

## Executive Summary:
Jawbreak is an iOS app demo that aims to help facial/oral surgery patients have peace of mind by tracking their recovery.
Jawbreak allows users to log facial swelling/tightness/numbness, by intensity, date, and with a picture.
Jawbreak also utilizes the iPhone’s TrueDepth camera to take a scan of a user’s face and provide metrics on their evolution throughout recovery.
Jawbreak stores all snapshots locally, adhering to privacy-first principles.

## Files
ContentView – app homepage & basic functions
CameraView – camera functionality
TruDepthScannerView – TrueDepth functionality for Facial Tracking/Metrics
JawBreakSimpleApp – app instance
EvolutionViewer – snapshot viewer and functionality
FaceScan – logging functionality and face metrics

## Notices
Jawbreak is downloadable via GitHub, and can be demo-ed with Xcode and a TrueDepth-capable iOS device running iOS 18 or higher.
